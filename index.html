ABOUT THE MUSIC

“Awɛ (My God) is a prayer, a celebration, and a homecoming. This song blends the soulful depth of Afro-jazz, the danceable energy of Amapiano, and the timeless catchiness of Afrobeats. Rooted in my Ghanaian heritage, I wanted to create something that speaks to the spirit while moving the body.”

— Salma A.D. Nabonadam

"Awɛ" means God in Kasem, a language spoken in parts of Ghana and Burkina Faso. The title reflects a core African belief in trusting God with life's path, struggles, and outcomes. Through this fusion of contemporary African sounds, I aim to honor tradition while pushing boundaries — making music that is both deeply personal and universally resonant.
